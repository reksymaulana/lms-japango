# Japango

### Learn Japanese, one step at a time.

Japango is a Japanese learning web app for students working toward **JLPT N5, N4, and N3**. Explore lessons, build your vocabulary, practice what you learn, and keep track of your progress.

## What you can do

| 📚 Learn | 🔎 Explore | ✍️ Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Study kanji, grammar, and learning materials. | Look up Japanese vocabulary and meanings. | Take quizzes and review with flashcards. |

### Make learning a habit

Use flashcards with spaced repetition to revisit words over time. Your dashboard also helps you follow your learning progress and review your past work.

## Made for students and teachers

- **Students** can explore published lessons, take exercises, and follow their own progress.
  Grammar patterns are complete when students mark them complete or pass a Sensei-linked
  quiz with at least 70% after grading.
- **Sensei (teachers)** can organize learning materials and quizzes, review student results, and provide feedback on written answers.

Multiple-choice questions are checked automatically. Written answers can be reviewed by a Sensei.

## Learn in your preferred language

The interface is available in **English** and **Indonesian**. Lesson content may be available in different languages.

---

**Japango is an open-source learning project.** Its lessons are a growing starting point for Japanese learners—not a complete JLPT curriculum.
